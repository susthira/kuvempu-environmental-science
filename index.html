The future is environmental

The world needs people who can understand change—and help solve it.

Climate, water, biodiversity, pollution and carbon are no longer distant concerns. They need environmental scientists who can observe, measure, analyse and act.

A living laboratory

Learn in one of India’s remarkable ecological landscapes.

Located in the Western Ghats region, Kuvempu University offers access to forest ecosystems and the Bhadra landscape. Study forests, biodiversity, soils, water, climate, vegetation and ecosystem processes where they occur.

Your landscape can become your laboratory.

From learning to doing

Do not only study science. Contribute to it.

Students can participate in research, collect field evidence, work with researchers and develop the confidence to communicate scientific work. Recent student participation in collaborative research has led to manuscript co-authorship.

Observe Measure Analyse Research Communicate Contribute

Science with tools

Measure the environment. Work with real data.

Gain exposure to instruments and approaches used in contemporary environmental research—from photosynthesis analysers and fluorometers to climate sensors, forest measurements and environmental monitoring.

From one leaf to an entire forest landscape.
